How registration works

Three steps, all powered by the charity events REST API.

1

Choose an event

Use the events list or the search page to filter by category, charity or keyword, then open the event to read the full description.

2

Register and pick tickets

The registration page collects your details and how many tickets you need. Your booking is saved with POST /api/events/:id/registrations.

3

Join the event

Your name appears in the event's registration list straight away, and the event's running total is updated automatically.
